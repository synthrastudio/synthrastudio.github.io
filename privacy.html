Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Synthra ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile applications published under the Synthra studio brand, including MoneyClicker (the "App"). This page describes how we handle information in connection with our Apps.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal information. All game progress is saved locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information (type, OS version, language)
  • Approximate location (country-level)
  • App interaction data for ad relevance

For details, see Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, we request your permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

No personal data is stored on our servers. Game progress is saved only on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at support@synthra.co.
